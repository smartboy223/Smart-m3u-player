How to use SignalDeck
  1. Paste M3U URLs and save.
  2. Select playlists, then Fetch & load.
  3. Browse or search their channels.
  4. Test the filtered list to see quality and previews.
  5. Click a channel to watch. Try compatibility mode if needed.

Protected, expired, or blocked streams may remain unavailable. Quality tests sample the stream at one moment.

Keys: Space to pause, F for fullscreen, M to mute, ↑/↓ for volume (when not typing).
